## Hypothesis
A 15% sitewide coupon reactivates lapsed customers at ≥ 2.5%.
## Result
Miss. 1.1% reactivation against 2.5%. Unsubscribe rate 0.46%, one tick from the guardrail.
## Surprise
The email with nothing to say about wine got the highest unsubscribe rate we have recorded. Lapsed customers did not leave because of price.
## Decision
- "Coupon-led winback underperforms and raises unsubscribes for lapsed; lead with the new vintage of something they bought" (candidate instinct)
- "Lapsed > 400 days should be a separate audience with a different hypothesis" (candidate instinct)
