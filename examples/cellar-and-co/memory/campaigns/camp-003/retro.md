## Hypothesis
Collectors sell through a 60-bottle allocation within 5 days when told the facts plainly and once.
## Result
Success. 100% sell-through in 31 hours, one email, 0.04% unsubscribes.
## Surprise
Demand was roughly 3× the allocation. We under-allocated and have no waitlist mechanism to capture the overflow.
## Decision
- "Plain, single-send allocation emails sell through for collectors; adjectives are unnecessary" (candidate instinct)
- "Allocation campaigns need a waitlist CTA for the sold-out state" (candidate instinct)
