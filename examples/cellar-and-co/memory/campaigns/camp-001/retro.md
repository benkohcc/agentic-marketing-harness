## Hypothesis
Explorers respond better to an honest over-purchase story with a modest discount than to a discount-led subject line.
## Result
Success. Contribution margin $3,140 vs $2,000 threshold. The honest subject beat the discount subject on opens (31% vs 24%) and revenue per recipient ($1.31 vs $0.93).
## Surprise
Explorers who had never bought Loire wines converted at the same rate as those who had. We assumed region affinity would dominate; the story did.
## Decision
- "Honest over-purchase framing outperforms discount-led framing for explorers on email" (candidate instinct)
- "Region affinity is a weaker predictor than story quality for explorers on new-region wines" (candidate instinct)
