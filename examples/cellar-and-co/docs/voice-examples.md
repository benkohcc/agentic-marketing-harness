# Cellar & Co. — voice examples

Anchors for the brand reviewer (rubric `brand_voice`). "This" examples are a 5; "Not
this" examples are a 1–2. Everything in between is the reviewer's judgment.

## Subject lines
- Not this: "🍷 HUGE Bordeaux Sale — Don't Miss Out!"
- This: "We bought more 2019 Médoc than we should have"
- Not this: "Discover Our Award-Winning Selection"
- This: "Nine wines our team re-ordered for themselves"

## Openers
- Not this: "As a valued customer, we're excited to share an exclusive offer…"
- This: "Our buyer went to Pauillac in March and came back with a problem: 90 cases."
- Not this: "Elevate your wine experience with our curated collection."
- This: "This is the bottle we open on a Tuesday when the chicken is already in."

## Tasting notes
- Not this: "A stunning, complex, elegant expression of terroir."
- This: "Cherries, a little pencil shaving, tannins that behave themselves."
- Not this: "Bursting with flavor and perfect for any occasion."
- This: "Lean, salty, and better cold than you'd expect. Oysters, or nothing."

## Offers
- Not this: "Save BIG with 20% OFF sitewide, limited time only!"
- This: "Until Sunday it's $4 a bottle less than it ought to be. Then it isn't."

## CTAs
- Not this: "Shop now" · "Click here" · "Learn more"
- This: "Reserve your six" · "Explore the Loire" · "See what's left"

## Collectors (allocation)
- Not this: "You've been specially selected for early access!"
- This: "Allocation is 3 bottles per customer. 60 bottles. Your window closes Thursday noon."
