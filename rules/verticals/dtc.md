# Vertical pack — direct-to-consumer e-commerce

Loaded via `compliance.packs`. Adds to rules/common; never overrides MARKETING.md.

## Checklist (compliance reviewer walks this per campaign)
- [ ] Price shown is the price charged; strikethrough "was" prices reflect a real prior
      price offered for a reasonable period.
- [ ] Discount percentage matches `brief.offer.discount_pct` exactly, everywhere.
- [ ] Offer end date is stated wherever the offer is.
- [ ] Shipping cost, threshold, or "free" claim matches current policy on file.
- [ ] Stock/scarcity statements ("only 40 left") are backed by current inventory data
      and the number is not rounded down for effect.
- [ ] Reviews or ratings quoted are real, current, and attributable.
- [ ] Subscription or auto-renew terms, if any, are stated before the CTA.
- [ ] Return policy is not contradicted or overstated.

## Strategist notes
- Winback and lapsed campaigns: a coupon trains customers to wait. Prefer product news
  with a small, time-boxed offer. Record which was used so retros can compare.
- Frequency: DTC lists fatigue fast. Default to the email cap; do not raise it for
  seasonal peaks without a guardrail on unsubscribe rate.

## Measurement notes
- Contribution margin must net out the discount and media spend. Revenue alone is not a
  success metric for a promotion.
- Attribution window default 7 days for email, 7-day click / 1-day view for paid.
