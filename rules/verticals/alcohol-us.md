# Vertical pack — alcohol, United States

Loaded via `compliance.packs`. This pack is a working checklist for the fictional
example business and a starting point for a real one. It is **not** legal advice; a real
deployment needs counsel to review and extend it. Items marked (L) force
`requires: legal_review` when triggered.

## Hard rules (compliance reviewer: fail = block)
- No health, therapeutic, or nutritional benefit claims of any kind. "Antioxidants,"
  "heart-healthy," "low-calorie" without the required statement — all prohibited.
- No content that depicts or appeals to anyone under 21: no cartoons, youth slang,
  school or campus settings, no Santa-with-wine.
- No association of drinking with driving, sports performance, or sexual success.
- No depiction of intoxication, rapid consumption, or drinking games.
- No "strong," "extra strength," or alcohol-content-as-benefit framing.
- No claims about shipping into a state unless that state is on the current
  ship-to list on file. (L) if the copy names a state.
- Sweepstakes, giveaways, free product. (L)
- Pre-order / futures terms. (L)
- "Organic," "biodynamic," "natural" only with producer certification on file.

## Audience rules
- Every audience export carries `age_verified: true` per row; preflight rejects otherwise.
- Paid social: platform minimum-age targeting set to 21 and alcohol category declared.
- No targeting by college, campus, or youth-affinity interests.

## Required elements (preflight checks these are in the template, not the copy)
- Responsible-drinking line in email footer.
- Age gate on every landing page.

## Writing notes
- Tasting notes are not claims about effects. "Cherries and tannins that behave" is fine;
  "the perfect way to unwind" is a mood-effect claim — avoid.
- Occasion framing is fine; "pairs with roast chicken" is the house style.

## Measurement notes
- Allocation and pre-order campaigns are measured on units, not revenue, to avoid
  rewarding price creep.
